How It Works

Core Rules of Tactical Mill (Cờ hàng 24)

Every match unfolds across two tactical phases where positioning, tempo, and mill locks determine victory.

1

Phase 1 — Nine-Stone Placement

Players alternate placing 9 stones onto any vacant intersection across the three concentric squares. Forming a straight line of 3 stones (a Mill) immediately lets you capture one unprotected opponent stone.

2

Phase 2 — Adjacent Movement & Shuttle Mills

Once all stones are placed, players slide one stone per turn along connected lines to an adjacent empty point. Opening and closing double mills creates decisive tactical forks.

3

Victory & Tournament Draw Rules

Win by reducing your opponent to 2 stones or blocking all of their legal moves. Competitive matches also enforce threefold position repetition and non-capture move-count draw rules.
