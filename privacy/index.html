Privacy Policy — Tactical Mill - Cờ hàng 24

Effective Date & Last Updated: November 24, 2026
Publisher / Data Controller: NexaloOnline (Nexalo)
Application: Tactical Mill - Cờ hàng 24 (Android package: online.nexalo.tacticalmill & Web application)
Contact Email: support@nexalo.online

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to operate online board-game matches, save your progress, and protect players against fraud or abuse:

  • Account & Profile Information: When you register a Nexalo ID, we collect your email address, username, display name, and an encrypted password hash (stored using scrypt; we never store plaintext passwords). If you play as a Guest, we generate a guest profile identifier without requiring an email address.
  • Device & Technical Identifiers: We generate and store an app-scoped Device ID (random UUID saved locally in app storage), along with connection metadata such as IP address and User-Agent in security and session logs to prevent multi-account reward abuse, enforce rate limits, and secure your sessions.
  • Gameplay & Virtual Economy Data: We record your match history, move replays (PGN), Glicko-2 rating points (RP), virtual currency balances and ledgers (virtual Coins, Gems, Tickets, Season Points), quest progress, and anti-cheat risk signals (move timing and match integrity checks).

Purchases and advertising. The Service may offer optional in-app purchases; payments are processed by Google Play and we do not receive or store your payment card details. The current version of the Service does not display advertising. If advertising is added in a future version, advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers to provide and measure ads, as described in Section 3, and this policy will be updated before or when that change takes effect.

Data We Do NOT Collect: We do not access or collect your precise GPS location, contacts, microphone, camera, photos, SMS, call logs, or health/biometric data.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to:

  • Authenticate your account, maintain active login sessions, and synchronize your game progress across devices;
  • Match you with opponents in real-time modes (Quick Match, Ranked, Arena, Friend Rooms) and calculate fair Glicko-2 ratings;
  • Send transactional account emails (such as password recovery codes or account deletion confirmations) when requested by you;
  • Verify entitlement receipts for optional in-app purchases processed by Google Play (when enabled), and — if advertising is enabled in a future update — deliver and measure ads in accordance with Section 1 and Section 3;
  • Detect and prevent cheating, bot automation, multi-account reward farming, and unauthorized account access.

3. Data Sharing, Service Providers & Cross-Border Processing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

We share information only with service providers that process it on our behalf to run the Service, such as cloud hosting providers and email delivery providers and — when those features are enabled — advertising partners. The app is distributed through Google Play, which also processes payments for in-app purchases. These providers may process data in countries other than yours. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We keep account and gameplay data while your account exists. You have the right to permanently delete your account and personal data at any time. When an account is deleted, data that belongs only to you is erased; records shared with other players (for example, match history) are anonymised so the other players' records remain intact, and security logs are kept for a limited period of 90 days for security and troubleshooting before being deleted or anonymised. Records created specifically for fraud prevention — such as risk assessments and enforcement actions on an account — may be kept longer, for as long as needed to protect the Service and other players; they are erased or anonymised when that need ends.

You can delete your account through either of two official channels:

  • In-App Instant Deletion: Open the app, tap your Account / Profile pill on the top bar, tap Delete Account, and confirm with your password (or confirmation phrase for Guest accounts). In-app deletion executes immediately.
  • Web Deletion Request (even if you uninstalled the app): Visit https://nexalo.online/account-deletion and submit a deletion request to support@nexalo.online from your registered email address (or provide your username/account ID). Verified web and email deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

What happens when your account is deleted:

  • Immediately Erased: Your email address, username, display name, password hash, active login sessions, refresh tokens, virtual wallet balances, ratings, quest progress, and personal profile state are permanently removed.
  • Anonymised Shared Records: To preserve the match history and ledger integrity of opponents you played against, shared match records and opponent-facing ledgers remain in the database with your display name replaced by generic text ("Deleted Player") and stripped of all personal identifiers.
  • Fraud-Prevention & Anti-Abuse Records: One-time device reward locks and anonymised device-level anti-fraud risk traces (keyed only by a cryptographic hash of the device identifier, without your email, username, user ID, or raw IP address) are retained as described above so the same device cannot repeatedly claim new-account welcome bonuses or evade fair-play protections.

5. Security

All network traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and WSS (WebSocket Secure). Passwords are hashed using memory-hard scrypt, and session refresh tokens use rotation with reuse detection.

6. Children's Privacy

Tactical Mill - Cờ hàng 24 is intended for players aged 13 and older (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact us at support@nexalo.online and we will promptly delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published at https://nexalo.online/privacy with an updated revision date. For privacy questions, data access requests, or deletion support, contact us at: support@nexalo.online.

4. Lưu trữ dữ liệu & Xoá tài khoản

Chúng tôi lưu giữ dữ liệu tài khoản và dữ liệu trò chơi trong suốt thời gian tài khoản của bạn tồn tại. Bạn có quyền yêu cầu xoá vĩnh viễn tài khoản và dữ liệu cá nhân của mình bất cứ lúc nào. Khi một tài khoản bị xoá, dữ liệu chỉ thuộc về riêng bạn sẽ bị xoá bỏ; các bản ghi dùng chung với người chơi khác (ví dụ: lịch sử trận đấu) được ẩn danh hoá để hồ sơ của người chơi khác giữ nguyên tính toàn vẹn, và nhật ký bảo mật được lưu giữ trong thời hạn giới hạn 90 ngày cho mục đích bảo mật và khắc phục sự cố trước khi bị xoá hoặc ẩn danh hoá. Các bản ghi được tạo riêng cho mục đích phòng chống gian lận — chẳng hạn như đánh giá rủi ro và biện pháp chế tài trên tài khoản — có thể được lưu giữ lâu hơn, trong khoảng thời gian cần thiết để bảo vệ Dịch vụ và những người chơi khác; chúng sẽ bị xoá hoặc ẩn danh hoá khi nhu cầu đó kết thúc.

Bạn có thể xoá tài khoản qua một trong hai kênh chính thức:

  • Xoá tức thì ngay trong ứng dụng: Mở ứng dụng, chạm vào nút Tài khoản / Hồ sơ trên thanh trên cùng, chọn Xoá tài khoản (Delete Account) và xác nhận bằng mật khẩu (hoặc cụm từ xác nhận đối với tài khoản Khách). Yêu cầu xoá trong ứng dụng được thực thi ngay lập tức.

Điều gì xảy ra khi tài khoản bị xoá:

  • Dữ liệu chung được ẩn danh hoá: Để không làm hỏng lịch sử đấu và sổ cái của các đối thủ từng thi đấu với bạn, biên bản trận đấu và sổ cái hai phía vẫn được giữ lại nhưng tên hiển thị của bạn được thay bằng chuỗi ẩn danh ("Deleted Player") và xoá sạch mọi định danh cá nhân.
